## 2. Defect register (everything the audit found)

### 2.1 Host-assignment parity bug (implementation; results documentation wrong)
`m4_driver.py` built tasks with `hosts[(j + sc) % 80]`; both `j` and `sc` increment per
task, so the walk has stride 2 over an even-length list — **only the 40 even-indexed of
the 80 drawn hosts were ever used** (verified: `recovery.csv` holds exactly 40 distinct
TICs, ~375 injections each). `M4_TEST_RESULT.md`, the paper's Table T1 ("over 80 test
hosts"), and the conditioning provenance narrative all said 80. **Impact:** halved host
noise diversity; every documented host count corrected to 40; E1 re-inferred with
40-cluster bootstrap (passes, §1.1). **Fix:** assignment is now `hosts[sc % len(hosts)]`
(full coverage) with a regression test (`tests/test_fast_units.py`).

### 2.2 E2 timing-rule deviation (protocol)
Frozen §6 rule vs executed run: 300-cap stratified vs **12 stars**; ≥5 repeats vs **1**;
CPU-core-seconds vs wall-clock. Additionally the timing loop **re-drew each injection
with a different RNG** (seed 7) than the recovery pass, so 3 of the 12 timed stars did
not even route in the re-run. **Fix:** driver defaults now implement the frozen rule
(≥10/cell, cap 300, 5 repeats, `time.process_time`, same per-row seed); the re-measurement
(§5) executes it on the sealed injection set.

### 2.3 Residual-space injection (protocol deviation; disclosed, not repaired)
`PHASE1_M4_PLAN.md` §3/step 4 specified *inject into raw PDCSAP, then condition*; the
sealed run used `host_mode="cached_residual"` — the mode `injection.py` itself labeled
"DRY-RUN" — so the M2-measured conditioning attenuation η was paid by **neither** arm.
Paired ΔR (E1) is unaffected; **absolute** recall figures (0.488/0.509 unweighted) are
optimistic and are now flagged wherever quoted. Constant LD (u₁=0.4, u₂=0.30) was used
instead of Claret-2017; the confirmer template used u₂=0.25 (mismatch, negligible).

### 2.4 τ_GP calibration/test inconsistency
M3 calibrated the period-FAP with **per-star** τ_GP; the M4 driver hardcoded 0.005 d for
every star. Masked in practice by L_b = 3·max(τ, T₁₄) with T₁₄ dominating; fixed for
future runs (per-star lookup); the re-measurement keeps 0.005 for sealed fidelity.

### 2.5 n_transits is formula-level
Eligibility used `floor(baseline/P)+1`, ignoring gaps and epoch. Data-driven counts from
the validated task reconstruction: **§4**. The sealed estimand is untouched (it is
defined on the formula); the "grid has zero monotransits" claim is downgraded to
formula-level and quantified.

### 2.6 π\* definition error
`endpoints.py` computed "π\*" as ρ_d / (search-cost fraction) = **0.236**; the theory
(MATH §8.3a) defines π\* = ρ_d / f_p with f_p the fraction of planet hosts taking the
cheap path. With the sealed run's f_p (confirmed-cheap) = 0.211 and ρ_d = 0.144, the
honest figure is **π\* ≈ 0.68** — i.e. the survey-scale break-even is ~3× worse than
reported. Corrected in `endpoints.py` (both quantities now reported under honest names).

### 2.7 E1 interval-method substitution
Sealed HYP §6 named Wilson/Clopper–Pearson per-cell combination; the code used a
percentile bootstrap. Both now computed (§1.1); conclusions identical.

### 2.8 Structural context for E1 (not a defect; a required disclosure)
92.8% of the frozen w_c sits on Rₚ≤2; **30.3% of total weight lies in cells with 0.000
recall in both arms** (ΔR ≡ 0 identically); **78.9% of injections took the fallback**,
where `rec_comb` is copied from `rec_tls`. Non-inferiority is therefore substantially
guaranteed by the fallback architecture + weighting; the cells where routing genuinely
operates (Rₚ=4–12) show losses up to −25.6 pp at <8% weight. Pre-registered and legal —
but any use of the E1 result must carry this framing.

### 2.9 The realized arbiter is the timing gate (framing correction)
With T_red = 0 (non-binding), Arm B's binding FP control is the **period-FAP gate — a
timing-coherence statistic** — plus sign/shape vetoes; the photometric Λ threshold does
no work at the sealed operating point (`TRED_CALIBRATION_RESULT.md` says so; the
headline docs did not). The M6 ablation quantifies it: removing the FAP gate takes null
FP 0.0% → 12.3%; removing shape/sign costs nothing on clean nulls. The charter's
"photometric significance, not timing coincidence, is the arbiter" is **not realized**
by the sealed Phase-I operating point; the paper's framing is corrected accordingly.

### 2.10 The rebrand silently broke the seal-verification chain (found during remediation)

## 4. Observed vs formula transit counts

Data-driven counts (distinct epochs with ≥1 in-transit cadence in the actual, gappy
time series) vs the sealed formula `floor(baseline/P)+1`, over all 15,000 injections:

- **892 injections (5.9%) counted as "≥2 transits" by the formula presented ≤1
  observed transit** — effective monotransits inside the headline estimand. They
  concentrate at P=16 d (852; 34% of that period's 2,500 injections) and P=8 d (40).
- 13,516 injections (90%) have observed < formula counts (median deficit 1–2 transits,
  from intra-sector gaps and epoch placement).

Both arms faced the identical injections, so the *paired* ΔR is unaffected; but the
claims "E1 estimand restricted to cells with n_transits ≥ 2" and "the grid contains
zero monotransits" (M7 consistency pass) hold only at the formula level, and absolute
completeness in the P=16 d cells is depressed by a ~34% effective-monotransit
admixture. The paper now states this.

## 6. Grid-edge control (the P = 0.5 d "gain region")

99.6% of Arm B's 563 sealed gains sit at P = 0.5 d — exactly the sealed TLS grid's
`period_min`. Control on cleaned CALIBRATION nulls (`edge_control.py`, 2026-07-19;
60 paired injections/condition, Rₚ = 8, 59 cleaned calibration null hosts, sealed
T = 10.74; artifacts `edge_control.{csv,json}`):

| Condition | recall | period_ok | SDE ≥ T | median SDE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A — P = 0.5 d on the sealed grid (period_min = 0.5) | 0.367 | 0.983 | 0.967 | 22.4 |
| B — same injections, grid extended to period_min = 0.3 | 0.367 | 0.983 | 0.967 | 22.4 |
| C — P = 0.62 d on the sealed grid | 0.983 | 0.983 | 0.983 | 25.7 |

**Grid-edge artifact NOT confirmed** — A and B are bit-identical per row (extending
the grid below the boundary changes nothing). But the control *replaces* the sealed
interpretation with a sharper one: full TLS at P = 0.5 d finds the right period at
high SDE (97–98%) and fails the recovery predicate almost entirely on **epoch** —
36 of the 38 A-condition failures are epoch-only (fitted T₀ outside ±0.5 T₁₄).
The sealed text's "the confirmer recovers planets whose full-grid SDE fell just
below T" is therefore wrong as a mechanism: the P = 0.5 d "gain region" is
predominantly a **T₀-precision failure of TLS at ultra-short periods** that Arm B's
detector-seeded epoch happens to survive — the mirror image of the 80%
right-period/wrong-epoch loss pathway. Both the gains and the majority of losses
are epoch-predicate phenomena, not detection-power differences.
